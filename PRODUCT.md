brand

## What this is

A single-page bilingual (EN/ES) portfolio for Leandro Trabucco — a full-stack developer based in Argentina with prior experience in Japan. Optimized for hiring managers, recruiters, and prospective freelance clients arriving from LinkedIn, GitHub, a resume PDF, or word of mouth.

## Audience

- **Engineering hiring managers and recruiters** evaluating for junior/mid full-stack and frontend roles, often remote. Read on desktop, skim hard, want stack legibility and shipped work within ~10 seconds.
- **Freelance/small-business clients** (similar to the El Alto cabin rental client) looking for someone who can build and own a small site or app end-to-end. Often Spanish-speaking, often on mobile.
- **Peers** linking to it from social or chat. Read fully out of curiosity. Hold the bar on craft.

About half of visits land on mobile. Both languages are equal-priority — neither is a translation of the other, both are the canonical version in their own right.

## Voice

Terminal-flavored, confident, light personality. The site leans into developer aesthetics without dressing up as a CLI: prompt-style markers (`$ whoami`, `$ ping <email>`), a `leo.json` identity card, a typewriter-animated hobby string. The tone is grounded, not boastful — accomplishments stated plainly with specific outcomes (e.g., "solving the problem of empty 'Hola' messages"). Bilingual copy stays in the same register in both languages; neither is more formal than the other.

Avoid: corporate platitudes ("passionate", "results-driven"), maximalist visual flexing, walls of buzzwords, generic SaaS gradient lingo.

## Success criteria

A visitor leaves the page having:
1. Understood what Leo builds with (React/Next.js/TypeScript + React Native + Rails + Supabase + PostgreSQL) and where he is (Argentina, formerly Japan).
2. Seen at least one recent project they could verify (Jidou Navi landing page, El Alto live site, Nihongo Hero PWA).
3. Either downloaded a resume in their preferred language OR clicked through to email/LinkedIn/GitHub.

Secondary: trusted the craft level enough to reach out rather than scroll past.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Blog or thought-leadership content.
- Contact form (mailto is intentional — friction is fine for the volume).
- Showcasing every project ever shipped (curation > completeness).
- Light mode (dark-only is intentional).
- Analytics dashboards or admin (deferred until there's a question that needs data to answer).
